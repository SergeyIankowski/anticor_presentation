## Access and coverage limitations

- **DINITROL:** HTTP 403 across browser, request API, HTTP/www variants and independent web fetch. Diagnostics and the actual failed parse report are committed. No services or prices were guessed. Once live content is accessible, service/vehicle/price selectors must be implemented and verified before publication can be enabled.
- **PROFICOR:** two optional pages (`/mojka/`, `/shumoizolyacziya/`) return empty HTTP 301 responses. The five successful pages were published; failures remain recorded.
- **Спец Антикор:** six linked detail/contact/guarantee URLs return HTTP 404. Homepage data and every calculator state were published; unavailable page details were not reconstructed.
- **Blasting.by:** `/contact/` returns HTTP 200 with page-not-found content. Homepage contacts and service evidence were published; the contact page is recorded as a soft-404.
- **LaserClean, Anticorlida, Anti-kor and Blasting.by:** no numeric tariffs are published on the inspected valid pages. Empty price arrays represent missing amounts, not free services.

Original qualifiers, ranges, units, material/vehicle labels, dates and source URLs are retained. Conflicting offers and source claims remain attributed and documented in each instance README. Алруса selects the dated schedule valid on the retrieval date. Different price units and vehicle scopes should be considered when comparing offers.
